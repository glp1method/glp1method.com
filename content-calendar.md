**Title:** Why Emotional Eating Doesn't Stop When the Food Noise Does
**URL:** /blog/emotional-eating-glp1
**Published:** May 2026
**Status:** Live ✓

**Target searches:** "emotional eating on Ozempic," "food noise vs emotional eating," "semaglutide still eating emotionally," "GLP-1 emotional eating"

**Core argument:** Food noise and emotional eating are different things. GLP-1s quiet the biological pull toward food — but emotional eating is a behavioral and psychological pattern serving a function (stress relief, comfort, boredom, numbing). - Research: emotional eating decreased at 3 months, returned to baseline at 12 months on GLP-1s without intervention
- What this means: the medication creates an easier environment to do the work, not a replacement for it
- Introduction of CBT framework without selling the workbook directly

## BONUS POST — Muscle Loss & Exercise Adherence
**Target month:** October or November 2026 (flag both as options; final call TBD)
**Title:** The Medication Doesn't Build the Habit. Here's What Does.
**URL:** /blog/glp1-muscle-loss-exercise-habits

**Target searches:** "GLP-1 muscle loss," "Ozempic exercise," "semaglutide losing muscle," "GLP-1 resistance training"

**Core argument:** Nearly all existing muscle-loss coverage in the GLP-1 space comes from dietitians and med spas talking about protein grams. The behaviorally significant finding, presented at ENDO 2026, is that physical activity measurably decreases in people on GLP-1 medications — that's a motivation/habit-formation problem, not a biology problem, and it sits squarely in the behavioral health lane with essentially no clinical-voice competition.

**Key beats:**
- Name the finding: activity drops on GLP-1s even though muscle preservation depends on it
- Why: appetite suppression removes a motivational cue, fatigue/GI symptoms during titration, and the "the drug is doing the work" mindset can unintentionally deprioritize movement
- The habit-formation angle: what actually gets people to start and sustain resistance training when the medication has quieted the usual hunger/body-dissatisfaction triggers people used to rely on for motivation
- Practical tool: pairing GLP-1 dosing weeks with a minimum-viable resistance training plan (not a fitness prescription — a behavior change one)
- Not medical advice — coordinate with prescriber, especially during dose titration

**Research to reference:**
- ENDO 2026 (Maharjan et al.) — physical activity decreases in GLP-1 users. endocrine.org/news-and-advocacy/news-room/2026/maharjan-press-release-endo-2026
- ADA 2026 muscle-preservation panel (AJMC coverage) — functional outcomes gap, muscle preservation strategies
- General context: lean mass contributes ~12-40% of total weight loss depending on agent (Medscape, July 2026)
